How it works

What to know before you click.

Where do the prices come from?

They come from recent searches by other travellers on a partner comparison site. They are checked twice a day and may have changed since.

Why can a price differ on the booking site?

Cheap seats go quickly, and baggage or extras can be added. When a check shows a price no longer exists, the flight is removed, but not every flight can be checked.

Is last minute cheaper?

Usually not: prices tend to rise as departure gets closer, although some routes are exceptions.

Can I search from another city?

Yes. The search works worldwide. The radar's deals leave from Paris for now.

How does the site make money?

Flight links are affiliate links. If you book after clicking, the site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Can I book directly here?

Not for now. Booking and payment happen on the partner's site, which also handles changes and refunds.

Where do the photos come from?

They come from Wikimedia Commons. The photographer and licence appear below the photos.
